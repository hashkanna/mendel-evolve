MendelEvolve

Genetics for algorithm discovery: evolve ideas, not programs, and measure what each idea is worth.

Every idea an LLM proposes becomes a gene: a named switch in the solver with a stated hypothesis. The LLM only invents genes. Classical search recombines and tunes them, and knockouts (switching one idea off, with paired seeds) measure what each idea is worth. The pages below are read-only snapshots of real runs.

Where those records came from

From the seed solver and compute, not from evolved ideas, and the ledger says so. One of the seed solver's own switches (build the set from antipodal pairs) moves the average run by a fifth of a point and doubles the share of runs that beat the published value: 262 of 1,600 with it, 136 of 1,600 without, on the same seeds and budgets. The engine's short screens could not see an effect that small, so every idea is being screened again at record-search scale; see RESULTS.md.

Other results

No isosceles triangles (problem 59). Two different 58-point sets in the 32 x 32 grid, where 56 is stated as the optimum. DeepMind's own verifier accepts them, and the same definition reproduces every published value from n = 4 to 10 by exhaustive search.

Circle packing, n = 26. Starting from OpenEvolve's initial program with no hints, Fable inventors reached the best known value, 2.635983. One gene carries it: its knockout costs 1.67.

Explaining another system. A program evolved by OpenEvolve, cut into five switches: one of them is 99% of the gain and two do nothing.
