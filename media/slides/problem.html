Sources: What Do Evolutionary Coding Agents Evolve? (arXiv:2605.20086) · Evolution or Illusion? (arXiv:2609.19799)

AlphaEvolve, OpenEvolve and their relatives have an LLM mutate whole programs and keep the high scorers. They find good algorithms, but afterwards nobody can say which idea produced the result. A study of their search traces this year found that about thirty percent of the lines they add are lines they had deleted earlier. They are Darwin without Mendel: selection, but no genes.
