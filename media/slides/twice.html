The honest part, which is the point. We turned the knockouts on our own records. The engine's quick screens said evolution had added nothing. That measurement was too weak to see a fifth of a point, so we repeated the knockouts at record scale: six hundred paired runs per arm, every pair in one container, on seeds the engine never saw. Three things showed up. One switch of the hand-written seed solver takes the share of record-beating runs from ten percent to thirty-four. The solver the engine evolved is a quarter of a point better. And one idea from an LLM inventor is worth more than anything the engine kept.
