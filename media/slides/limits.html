What it cannot do yet

Quick screens are too weak

48 seed pairs at 45 seconds resolve about 0.2 points. Everything that mattered here was that size or smaller.

It selects on the mean

Records live in the tail. A seventh of a point on the mean more than triples the share of record-beating runs.

The ledger does not yet steer

Four runs with inventors reading it, four without: 0.872 against 0.903. It explains a result. It did not improve the search.

Pairing must include the machine

Our first deep screen ran arms in separate containers and showed eight effects that were not real. We say so in the results.

Next: select on the rate of runs above a target, and screen with more seeds where an interval still spans zero.
