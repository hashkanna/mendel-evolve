In MendelEvolve every idea an LLM proposes becomes a gene: a named switch in the solver with a stated hypothesis. The LLM only invents genes. Classical search recombines and tunes them, with no LLM calls. Then we do what geneticists do: knock each gene out, singly and in pairs, on seeds the tuner never saw, and measure what the solver loses.
